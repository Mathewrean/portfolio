# Why these notes live here

Medium started hiding lab writeups behind walls I do not control. A share link should open the note, not a signup page.

This catalog is the replacement:

- categories for platform and research
- terminals that stay monospace and copyable
- one reaction and one star per browser
- a read counter shared across visitors
- a URL you can send: it lands on this site

New notes are drafted in **New writeup**, then committed as markdown plus `data/writeups.json`.
